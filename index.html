Soccer, redefined.

Kick Kings is a competitive mobile soccer game built from the ground up for the modern player. Skill-based matchmaking. Real-time multiplayer. A deep card-collection system. And a global ranking ladder that crowns one champion every season.

Whether you live for the last-minute screamer or grind ranked all night, Kick Kings is built for the obsessed. Pick up a controller — or just your phone — and start your climb.

Inside the Game

Built for the obsessed.

Every system in Kick Kings is designed around one question: does this make competitive play more rewarding?

⚔️

Real-time 1v1 & 5v5

Skill-based matchmaking pairs you against players at your level. No more lopsided matches — every game is a war.

👑

Global ranked ladder

Climb from Bronze to King. Ten ranked tiers, weekly resets on the leaderboard, and a champion crowned at the end of every 30-day season. Top 100 get their tag etched into the in-game stadium.

🏆

Daily challenges

Fresh objectives every 24 hours. Earn XP, packs, and limited drops you can't get any other way.

📱

Mobile-first, 60 FPS

Built native for iOS and Android. Console-quality graphics, sub-50ms touch response. No lag, no excuses.

Clubs & tournaments

Form a club, run weekly tournaments with cash prize pools, and rep your crew on the global stage.
